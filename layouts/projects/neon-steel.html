Production and workflow

Keeping 20 people moving in the same direction

With people working across programming, design, art, animation, audio, narrative, and UI, a large part of my job was making sure everyone knew what needed to happen next. I planned milestones, set priorities, tracked dependencies, followed up on blockers, ran reviews, coordinated playtests, and organized the work around launch.

Scope changed as the project developed. I worked with the discipline leads to compare the cost of each feature with the time and people we had available. That let us protect the core experience and complete the approved release scope two weeks before launch.

I also worked with the art leads and Carl Spidla to plan when combat, tutorials, enemies, weapons, dialogue, and new mechanics would appear. The pacing board helped us connect the rise in gameplay pressure with Kade's characterization instead of treating those as separate tracks.

Approved release scope

Core combat, HUD, save and load, grappling hook, weapons, pickups, settings, hazards, and enemy navigation.

Deferred features

Codex and audio log pickups, a more interactive Neon interface, an animated liquid Neon bar, a specialized mech HUD, additional accessibility options, and other secondary mechanics.

To make the daily workflow easier to manage, I spent about 20 hours researching Trello automation and building separate pipelines for development and art. The automations created and updated checklists, moved cards, applied labels, handled evaluation and testing states, and prepared completed work for archival. This reduced recurring board administration and gave each discipline a consistent path from active work to completion.

HUD redesign

Putting essential information in one place

Problem
The original HUD spread vital information across several areas of the screen.
Decision
I consolidated health, ammunition, abilities, and Neon into one shared interface area.
Implementation
I designed and built the revised HUD in Unreal Engine using Blueprints.
Result
Players could check the information they needed without searching across the viewport.
